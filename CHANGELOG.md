# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Ranks the same Wikipedia article across its leading language versions.
- Measures prose words, excluding references and navigation.
- Counts references as a separate signal of how well sourced each version is.
- Reads Featured and Good article badges from Wikidata, worth up to 5% of the score.
- Adjusts word counts for how compactly each language writes.
- Names the best language to read, or marks the one you are on when nothing scores higher.
- Flags the best-sourced version when it differs from the overall winner.
- Offers a translate link when the winner is a language you do not read.
- Balanced, Most complete and Best sourced presets, plus a fine slider.
- Measures 6, 12 or 24 languages exactly.
- Light, dark and automatic themes, with seven accent colors.
- Drag the card anywhere on the page; it remembers where you left it.
- Minimize it to a pill, or close it for that page.
- Card text in 20 languages, following the article's language.
- Settings labels in 20 languages, with the longer explanations still in English.
- Alt+Shift+K opens the panel, Alt+Shift+E turns it on or off.
- Full keyboard navigation, visible focus, and reduced-motion support.
- Says what went wrong and offers Retry when Wikipedia cannot be reached.
- Support tab writes a bug report for you, as a GitHub issue or an email.
- Export, import and reset your settings.
- No server, no account, no tracking.
